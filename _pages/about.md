Hi, I'm a PhD student at [ELLIS Institute Tübingen](https://tue.ellis.eu) and the [Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems (MPI-IS)](https://is.mpg.de/en), where I am advised by [Rediet Abebe](https://www.redietabebe.com/). 

My research focuses on AI alignment, continous learning and  distillation. More specifically, I'm asking how continous knowledge & value transfer (AI-AI, Human-AI) works and can be improved.

I hold a B.Eng. and an M.Sc. in Computer Science from TU Munich, and completed my Master’s thesis at Stanford University under the supervision of [Sanmi Koyejo](https://cs.stanford.edu/~sanmi/) ([Stanford Trustworthy AI Research (STAIR) Lab](https://stairlab.stanford.edu), [Stanford Human-Centered AI](https://hai.stanford.edu)). Before pursuing my graduate studies, I worked as an R&D Engineer at Mercedes-Benz. There, I was part of the special projects team that developed the [Vision EQXX](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/technology/innovation/vision/vision-eqxx.html), a record-breaking electric concept car that achieved over 1,000 km on a single charge.

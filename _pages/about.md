Hi, I'm a doctoral scholar at the ELLIS Institute Tübingen and the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, where I am advised by Rediet Abebe. My research focuses on AI alignment and knowledge distillation. I hold a B.Eng. and an M.Sc. in Computer Science from TU Munich, and completed my Master’s thesis at Stanford University's Human-Centered AI Lab under the supervision of Sanmi Koyejo. Before pursuing my graduate studies, I worked as an R&D Engineer at Mercedes-Benz. There, I was part of the special projects team that developed the Vision EQXX, a record-breaking electric concept car that achieved over 1,000 km on a single charge.

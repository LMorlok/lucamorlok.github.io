Hi, I’m a PhD student at ELLIS Institute Tübingen and the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems (MPI-IS), where I am advised by Rediet Abebe.

My research focuses on AI alignment, continous learning and distillation. More specifically, I’m asking how continous knowledge & value transfer (AI-AI, Human-AI) works and can be improved.

I hold a B.Eng. and an M.Sc. in Computer Science from TU Munich, and completed my Master’s thesis at Stanford University under the supervision of Sanmi Koyejo (Stanford Trustworthy AI Research (STAIR) Lab, Stanford Human-Centered AI). Before pursuing my graduate studies, I worked as an R&D Engineer at Mercedes-Benz. There, I was part of the special projects team that developed the Vision EQXX, a record-breaking electric concept car that achieved over 1,000 km on a single charge.
